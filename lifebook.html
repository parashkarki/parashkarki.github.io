Life Manual 📜
The rules I live by.
Version 1.0

Parash Karki

21 years old · Came to South Korea to build a better future.
My current job is not my final destination.
Every day I work is another step toward becoming the man I want to be.

🎯 My Purpose ›
  • I will build a healthy body.
  • I will learn Korean until I can speak confidently.
  • I will become a skilled programmer and app developer.
  • I will improve my English.
  • I will save money wisely.
  • I will become financially independent.
  • I will make my family proud.
🌅 My Daily Rules ›
  • I wake up with gratitude.
  • I make my bed.
  • I drink 500 mL of water.
  • I never skip breakfast.
  • I drink 3.5–4.5 liters of water throughout the day.
  • I eat enough food to support muscle growth.
  • I include protein in every main meal.
  • I do my work honestly and safely.
  • I treat people with respect.
  • I keep learning every day.
  • I sleep at least 7 hours whenever possible.
🍽️ My Nutrition Rules ›
  • My goal is to reach 65 kg.
  • Food is fuel.
  • I eat three main meals every day.
  • I add healthy snacks if needed.
  • I eat eggs, chicken, fish, rice, vegetables, fruit, milk, yogurt, oats, peanuts, and other nutritious foods regularly.
  • If I use a mass gainer, it supports my meals — it does not replace them.
  • If I use creatine, I take 5 grams every day.
  • I avoid skipping meals because of a lack of appetite.
🏋️ My Workout Rules ›
  • I train three days every week.
  • I focus on good technique.
  • I gradually increase the weight or repetitions.
  • I stretch after training.
  • I don't quit because I'm tired.
📚 My Learning Rules ›
  • I practice Korean every day.
  • I improve my programming skills every week.
  • I build projects instead of only watching tutorials.
  • I read something useful every day.
  • I ask questions when I don't understand.
💰 My Money Rules ›
  • I save part of every paycheck.
  • I avoid buying things I don't need.
  • I invest in my education and health.
  • I remember that money is a tool, not the goal.
🧠 My Mindset ›
  • I do not compare myself to others.
  • I compare myself to who I was yesterday.
  • I accept that progress takes time.
  • I stay patient.
  • I stay disciplined.
  • I finish what I start.
❤️ My Health ›
  • I drink enough water.
  • I protect my body at work.
  • I take care of my hair and skin.
  • I sleep enough.
  • I rest when I need recovery.
✅ My Weekly Checklist ›
🔑 Things I Will Never Forget ›
  • My job is not my identity.
  • Being tired is normal.
  • Quitting is a choice.
  • Small improvements become big results.
  • Consistency beats motivation.
  • Health is my greatest investment.
  • Knowledge creates opportunities.
  • Time is my most valuable resource.
"

One day I will have a strong and healthy body. I will speak Korean confidently. I will work in a technology career that I enjoy. I will create apps that help people. I will continue learning throughout my life. I will support my family.

I will be proud of the man I became — not because life was easy, but because I kept moving forward. 🚀

"I don't need to be perfect today.
I only need to do today's work.
If I keep doing the right things every day, my future will change."
